the idea

Before summer disappears, let's spend an afternoon outside looking at the little pieces of our lives.

No Small Photos is a gathering about getting to know people through the photographs they keep.

Not the photos you post.
Not the photos you take for anyone else.
The ones that somehow stayed.

a blurry night.
someone you love.
a place you almost forgot about.
a completely ordinary tuesday.

A photograph can be a very small doorway into a much bigger story.

photos from our last no small talk in the park Sept 12th ↓

your part

bring 5–6 printed photographs from your life.

They can be old or new, beautiful or terrible, meaningful or completely mundane.

  • a photo you love
  • a photo you almost deleted
  • a photo with a story nobody would guess
  • a completely ordinary moment
one important thing:

at least one of your photographs should be a photo you're happy to cut, fold, glue, or otherwise lovingly destroy.

don't bring anything irreplaceable.
what we'll do

We'll spread out our photographs and start getting curious about each other.

You'll meet people through their pictures, move around, answer some good questions, play a little, and make something with your hands.

You don't need to come with a friend.

In fact, coming alone is encouraged.

then we make

At some point, we'll stop talking and start making.

We'll turn photographs, playing cards, scraps, questions and little pieces of the afternoon into tiny handmade books.

Something to take home.

A little record that we were here.

materials included:
  • playing cards
  • scissors
  • glue
  • paper + other little things
