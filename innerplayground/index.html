small prompts. big adventures.

A little card game for curious adults.

welcome to inner playground.

The only rule? Be curious.


There are no right answers here.


Every card is an invitation to notice, imagine, create, or connect.


Some questions will make you laugh. Some might surprise you. Some might stay with you long after you've put the cards away.

34 prompt cards
2 instruction cards
1 little bag

ways to play

Play alone or play together. Choose a card, then choose how you want to respond.

tell a story
Turn the prompt into a story.
draw it
Draw whatever comes to mind.
use only three words
Three words. That's it.
write it
One sentence each.
time it
Make everyone respond in under 60 seconds.
answer as your 8-year-old self
Let your younger self answer.
answer as your future self
Answer from somewhere in the future.
combine them
Make up your own combination.
cambio

Change one or more words in the question to make up a new one.

wildcard

Make up your own way to play.
