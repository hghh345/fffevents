welcome to inner playground.

The only rule? Be curious.


There are no right answers here.


Every card is an invitation to notice, imagine, create, or connect.


Some questions will make you laugh. Some might surprise you. Some might stay with you long after you've put the cards away.

one more thing...

Inner Playground has been years in the making.


I've tested different versions, played with different prompts, and kept coming back to one simple idea:


we could all use more ways to connect, with each other and with ourselves.


I created Inner Playground as a way to make those connections feel a little easier, more playful, and a lot less predictable. Because sometimes the right question can take you somewhere you didn't expect.
